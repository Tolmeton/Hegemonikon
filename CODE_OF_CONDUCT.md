# Code of Conduct

This project is small and deliberate. The conduct it asks for follows from how it works, not from a template.

## What we expect

- **Argue propositions, not persons.** Critique the claim, the derivation, the source — never the contributor.
- **Cite your sources.** Distinguish what you observed from what you inferred. Unsourced certainty is treated as a defect, not an insult.
- **Accept refutation.** A position that is shown to be wrong is corrected, not defended for its own sake. This applies to maintainers as much as to contributors.
- **Be honest over agreeable.** Polite disagreement that improves the work is more valued here than friction-free assent.

## What is unacceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning or discriminatory remarks, in any venue connected to the project.
- Bad-faith argument: deliberate misreading, moving goalposts, or asserting authority in place of reasons.
- Publishing others' private information.

## What this is not

This Code of Conduct governs **conduct**, not permitted opinions. Holding an unconventional or uncomfortable position is not a violation; how you conduct yourself while holding it is what matters. The project's aim is a high standard of intellectual honesty, not ideological conformity.

## Enforcement

Concerns may be raised privately with the maintainer through the channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). The maintainer will review in good faith and respond as the situation requires, up to and including removing a contributor from the project's spaces. As a single-maintainer project, responses are best-effort.
