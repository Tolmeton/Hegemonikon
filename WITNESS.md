# Witness: Hegemonikón Governing Its Own Launch

> **English** | [日本語](WITNESS.ja.md)

"Hegemonikón uses Hegemonikón to launch Hegemonikón" is not a slogan. It is a constraint applied to this repository's own publication: before anything here went public, HGK's own cognitive laws — the 12 Nomoi (see [OVERVIEW.md](OVERVIEW.md) §9) — were run over the act of publishing.

This is a curated witness of that process. It is not a raw log, and it is not a safety claim.

## The chain

### 1. SOURCE before claim (N-01 / N-09)

The overview's account of the axiom layer asserted one ordering. Rather than trust it, it was checked against the canonical system core — which had since moved on. The public framing was corrected to match (the Helmholtz decomposition as the L0 axiom, the Free Energy Principle as its first instance) **before** publication, not after.

### 2. Verify identity against the primary record (N-10)

The citation metadata required an identifier. It was found in a single secondary record whose primary source was missing. Instead of publishing on that basis, the gap was surfaced and the value confirmed with its owner before it entered a public file.

### 3. Boundary before exposure

The public overview was derived from an internal document. Internal file paths, runtime tool names, and implementation detail were stripped under a no-local-path gate, and internal-only sections were dropped, before the public edition was written.

### 4. Confirm before the irreversible (N-04)

Publishing is irreversible. Every push to the public branch was preceded by an explicit statement of exactly what would become public, and proceeded only on confirmation. The main line of work was never pushed.

### 5. Reject what dilutes

A proprietary, still-internal theorem was deliberately **not** placed in the public-facing material — kept for internal documents until it earns public exposure, rather than shipped for effect.

## What this is — and is not

- It **is** a documented witness that HGK's laws were applied to HGK's own release.
- It is **not** a claim that HGK "prevents hallucination."
- It is **not** a claim that the project is ready for launch.
- It is **not** a benchmark.
- The private workspace is **not** the demo; this curated chain is.
